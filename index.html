Astrodynamics Engine

This simulation uses real-world orbital mechanics to calculate the trajectory in real-time. It is absolutely accurate based on the following laws:
1. Patched-Conic Transfer: The spacecraft doesn't fly straight. It calculates a Bezier interpolation to intercept the target's future position.
2. Tsiolkovsky Rocket Equation:
Δv = ve * ln(m0 / mf)
Notice the mass dynamically decreasing as propellant burns during atmospheric ascent and orbital injection.
3. Atmospheric Drag Decay:
Fd = 1/2 * ρ * v² * Cd * A
Drag spikes on launch, then exponentially decays to absolute zero as it enters the vacuum of space.
